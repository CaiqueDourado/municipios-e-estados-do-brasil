What was found while profiling the raw export, and what was done about it. Every fix is code in `scripts/build.py`; `raw/` keeps the original values.

## Checked and correct (do not "fix")

- **9 IBGE codes fail the usual check-digit rule:** 2201919 Bom Princípio do Piauí, 2201988 Brejo do Piauí, 2202251 Canavieira, 2611533 Quixabá, 3117836 Cônego Marinho, 3152131 Ponto Chique, 4305871 Coronel Barros, 5203939 Buriti de Goiás, 5203962 Buritinópolis. They are the official codes; the validator allows exactly these.
- **3 municipalities use the neighbouring state's area code:** Barracão (PR) uses 49, Rio Negro (PR) uses 47 and Porto União (SC) uses 42. They are twin border towns that share a phone area with Dionísio Cerqueira (SC), Mafra (SC) and União da Vitória (PR). Every other DDD matches its state, and the validator checks this.
- **5,570 rows** is the full IBGE list: 5,568 municipalities, plus the Federal District (Brasília) and the state district of Fernando de Noronha.
- **Coordinates** were compared with each state's centre: no municipality falls outside its state's plausible range. The farthest is Fernando de Noronha (PE), 786 km offshore, as expected.

## Enrichment notes

- **No CPTEC id in v1.2:** the CPTEC/INPE city list could not be downloaded (BrasilAPI HTTP 500, CPTEC HTTP 403, October 2026). The explorer looks the forecast up by name instead.
- **`elevation_m`** is the terrain elevation at the seat coordinate, from a 90 m model. It can differ from the official altitude of the town centre by tens of metres.
- **Hydrography (`hydro_*`)** comes from FBDS high-resolution mapping (satellite imagery, scale around 1:25,000). Values describe the mapping, not field surveys; small streams under the image resolution are missing, and dates differ by state (`hydro_source_updated`). Coverage: 5,470 of 5,570 municipalities (98.2%). The other 100 have no folder in the FBDS repository (RS 94, MT 3, MS 2, PE 1) and stay empty: almost all are in the Pampa (southern half of Rio Grande do Sul, including Porto Alegre and Pelotas) and in the Pantanal (Corumbá, Ladário, Poconé, Barão de Melgaço, Santo Antônio do Leverger), plus the island of Fernando de Noronha; the list is in [hydro-missing.csv](hydro-missing.csv), regenerated by `build.py`.
- **FBDS quirks handled by the crawler:** 6 municipalities (Bragança Paulista, Nazaré Paulista, Piracaia, Rafard and Suzano in SP; Camanducaia in MG) store polygon rings in the opposite direction, which gave negative areas until the crawler started using the absolute area of each polygon; 3 folders in Goiás also hold a file of a neighbouring municipality, so the IBGE code is taken from the file whose municipality name matches the folder; Sergipe has 4 empty duplicate folders with spaces in the name next to the real ones with underscores.
- **Municipalities created after this table:** the IBGE APIs may list municipalities that are not in the 5,570 rows (for example, Boa Esperança do Norte, MT). `build.py` reports them and leaves them out until they get coordinates, DDD and SIAFI code.

## Known limitations

- **Coordinates** have variable precision (0 to 6 decimals); Carvalhos (MG) has `-22` with no decimals. The seat location, not the centroid.
- **State coordinates** are rough centres with 2 decimals.
- **Western Pará** uses `America/Belem`; the IANA database also has `America/Santarem` for that area, with identical rules since 2008.
- **Western Amazonas** follows the raw offsets: 13 municipalities at UTC-5, including Lábrea and Jutaí.
- **DDD** is the main code of the municipality; a few municipalities are served by more than one.
- **Name spelling** follows the source (`Alta Floresta D'Oeste`, `Itapejara d'Oeste`); IBGE itself is not uniform on `D'`/`d'`.
